## Design Context

### Users

Fellow software engineers, DevOps practitioners, and technical leaders looking
for practical content on Go, PHP, containers, cloud infrastructure, and AI. They
visit to read blog posts, learn about Jason's experience, and find speaking
appearances. Context: focused reading, often on desktop, sometimes mobile.

### Brand Personality

**Technical, approachable, personal.** Competent and opinionated but calm and
inviting. Not corporate, not trying too hard — and not costume-nerdy.

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Tone**: Quiet personal Laravel-dev blog. Sans-first, bold titles, calm chrome.
- **Reference**: freek.dev — Whitney-like sans, heavy headings, coral accent
  rules, pill tags, generous spacing (not the three-column layout).
- **Anti-references**: Terminal mockups, prompt prefixes, DaisyUI retro,
  serif costume for “editorial,” flashy motion, generic SaaS landings.
- **Palette**: Cool paper `#F6F7FB` / deep blue-black dark `#0B0F16`, muted
  blue-gray text, rose accent `#D4545A` (light) / `#F08186` (dark). Soft wash
  gradients and tinted tags — noticeable, not loud.
- **Type**: IBM Plex Sans (stand-in for Whitney) everywhere; IBM Plex Mono for
  code. Extra-bold post titles.

### Design Principles

1. **Readability first** — Comfortable for 5–10 minute reads
2. **Content-forward** — Design disappears when reading
3. **Quiet chrome** — Header, footer, and links stay out of the way
4. **Restrained color** — Ink, paper, one muted accent — nothing loud
5. **Respect the reader** — Accessible, fast, no tricks
